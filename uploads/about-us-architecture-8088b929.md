**版本:** v1.0 | **日期:** 2026-04-23  
**说明:** 中文为内部理解标注，英文为页面实际展示文案（草稿级）

## 一、用户路径分析 User Path Analysis

### 三类主要访客

---

**路径 A｜评估型访客 (The Evaluator)**

> 中文：已经看过 Landing Page，想搞清楚"这是谁做的、可不可信"，再决定要不要注册。

- 来源：导航栏 About → 或 Footer 链接
- 核心问题：*"这个产品背后有人在认真做吗？他们的判断和我对齐吗？"*
- 期待在页面上找到：清晰的使命 + 产品是什么 + 为什么这个方向是对的
- 转化目标：注册 / 开始免费试用

---

**路径 B｜媒体 / 合作型访客 (Press & Partner)**

> 中文：在写报道或谈合作，需要标准的公司定位介绍，以及联系方式。

- 来源：搜索 "aisee.live" / 他人分享链接
- 核心问题：*"这家公司是做什么的？我怎么联系他们？"*
- 期待在页面上找到：产品定位 + 一句话介绍 + 联系入口
- 转化目标：发送合作/采访邮件

---

**路径 C｜社区早期用户 (Early Adopter)**

> 中文：已经在用产品，想了解背后是谁、价值观是否对味，顺便看看能不能跟上动态。

- 来源：产品内 About 链接 / X (Twitter) 主页跳转
- 核心问题：*"这个团队在乎什么？我们是同类人吗？"*
- 期待在页面上找到：价值观 + 做这个的原因 + 社区入口
- 转化目标：关注 X 账号 / 加入社区

---

共 **6 个区块（Sections）**，无 Team、无 Stats（当前阶段跳过）。

| # | 区块名称（中文） | Section Name (EN) | 优先级 | 说明 |
|---|--------------|-------------------|--------|------|
| 1 | 主视觉 / 使命区 | Hero + Mission | 🔴 核心 | 第一屏，定调整张页面 |

## 三、信息层级 Information Hierarchy

---

### Section 1｜Hero + Mission
> 中文：页面第一屏。使命宣言 + 一句产品解释。让访客在 5 秒内明白"这是什么、为什么重要"。

```
L1 [Headline — Mission Statement]
   "Visibility used to mean Google. Now it means AI. We handle both."

L2 [Sub-headline — Product Brief, 1 sentence]
   Aisee is an all-in-one GEO toolkit that helps brands and projects
   become visible, trusted, and recommended by AI engines.

L3 [Optional Tag / Label]
   Building for the AI-first internet  ·  Est. [year]
```

---

### Section 2｜The Context（为什么现在）
> 中文：用 3 段短文讲清楚"为什么 GEO 这件事现在必须做"。这一块建立行业认知，让访客觉得 Aisee 理解这个转变。

```
L1 [Section Label]
   Why This Matters

L2 [Opening Statement — 1 sentence]
   Search is no longer just Google.

L3 [Body — 3 short paragraphs / points]

   Point 1 — The shift
   "People are asking ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI assistants
   instead of typing into a search bar. The gatekeepers changed."

   Point 2 — The problem
   "Most brands and projects are invisible to these AI engines —
   not because they're bad, but because they haven't been
   optimized for how AI reads and recommends content."

   Point 3 — The opportunity
   "GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the new SEO.
   The brands that figure this out now will own the next decade
   of discovery."
```

---

### Section 3｜What We Build（我们在做什么）
> 中文：产品是什么 + 核心能力 4 项。不深入技术，控制在让非技术决策者也能看懂的粒度。

```
L1 [Section Label]
   What We Build

L2 [Product Statement — 1 sentence]
   A toolkit that scans, tracks, and improves how AI engines
   perceive and recommend your brand.

L3 [4 Core Capabilities — icon + label + 1-line description]

   → Scan
     Reveal how AI engines currently see your brand —
     knowledge gaps, trust issues, citation blind spots.

   → Track
     Monitor your AI visibility score across multiple engines
     from a single dashboard.

   → Optimize
     Get prioritized, actionable recommendations tied to
     the highest-impact GEO ranking factors.

   → Measure
     Track real improvement: ranking shifts, traffic growth,
     sentiment changes, and conversions.
```

---

### Section 4｜Who It's For（为谁而做）
> 中文：目标用户画像，让访客快速对号入座。3 类人群，每类一句话说清楚场景。

```
L1 [Section Label]
   Who It's For

L2 [3 User Types — label + scenario]

   Founders & Indie Makers
   "You built something great. Now make sure AI finds it."

   Marketing & Growth Teams
   "Your SEO is solid. Your AI visibility is a black box. Let's fix that."

   Agencies & Consultants
   "Add GEO to your service stack. Deliver measurable AI visibility
   results for clients."
```

---

### Section 5｜Our Principles（我们的原则）
> 中文：3-4 条价值观。不是企业文化套话，是真正影响产品决策的判断标准。建立信任 + 吸引同类人。

```
L1 [Section Label]
   How We Think

L2 [3–4 Principles — title + 2-line description]

   AI-first, not AI-hype
   We track what actually affects AI recommendations —
   not trends, not shortcuts, not black-box promises.

   Visibility with substance
   Getting noticed by AI means being genuinely useful,
   structured, and trustworthy. We help you earn it.

   Simple metrics, real results
   We don't show you dashboards for the sake of dashboards.
   Every number connects to a decision you can make.

   Built for builders
   Whether you're a solo founder or a growth team,
   the workflow should feel fast and obvious.
```

---

### Section 6｜What's Next（行动入口）
> 中文：多 CTA 并排，满足三类访客的不同需求。不强制漏斗，给访客选择权。

```
L1 [Section Label / Closing Line]
   Ready when you are.

L2 [4 CTAs — 并排或分组排列]

   [Primary CTA]
   Start Free →
   "Try Aisee — no credit card required"

   [Secondary CTA]
   Explore Features →
   "See exactly what's inside"

   [Contact CTA]
   Get in Touch →
   "For partnerships, press, or questions"
      contact: [your email / contact form link]

   [Community CTA]
   Follow on X →
   "Catch updates, GEO insights, and what we're building"
      link: [your X handle]
```
