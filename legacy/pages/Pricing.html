Billing & credits FAQ

Are Analysis, Engage and Post Agent credits separate?

No — all three modules share the same monthly pool. Spend it however you need: heavy on Analysis, heavy on AI replies, heavy on publishing, or any mix.

Do unused credits roll over?

Plan credits expire at the end of each billing cycle. Top-up pack credits never expire and are auto-used only after your plan credits run out.

What happens if I run out mid-month?

Fixed-cost actions (publish, tracking) are blocked with a "balance low" prompt. Token-based actions (Analysis, AI gen) warn before running. Buy a top-up pack to keep going.

Why are token-based costs estimated?

Analysis and AI content gen cost depends on actual model usage (prompt + response tokens). We show an average, but the final deduction reflects real consumption.

Is Post Agent really on all plans?

Yes. Every plan includes Post Agent on all platforms. Plans differ in account limits, monthly post cap, data tracking depth, and the Performance Report dashboard (Pro only).

Can I downgrade?

Yes. Your current cycle continues as-is. New limits apply next cycle. Sent history and analytics stay accessible read-only.
